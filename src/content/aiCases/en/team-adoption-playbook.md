title: Team-level AI adoption with practical guardrails
summary: AI is introduced as an engineering support tool with explicit constraints, repeatable prompts, and quality expectations instead of as a novelty layer.
challenge: Adoption fails when usage is vague, unreviewed, or disconnected from the team workflows that actually need improvement.
workflow:
  - Document repeatable prompt patterns for summarizing tickets, comparing approaches, and preparing refactors.
  - Keep humans responsible for validation, production decisions, and business-context checks.
  - Share examples that show when AI helps, when it does not, and how to detect weak output quickly.
outcome: The team gets practical acceleration without weakening technical judgment or code ownership.
metrics:
  - value: Shared usage patterns
    label: More consistent prompts and review expectations across collaborators
  - value: Higher signal usage
    label: AI applied to concrete engineering steps instead of generic experimentation
